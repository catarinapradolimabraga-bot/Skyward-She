New Content & What’s Coming to Skyward She

Published on December 14, 2025

Skyward She is growing — and this update marks an important step in the evolution of the site.

New content has been added to Aerospace 101, expanding explanations on aerospace fundamentals and making the section more complete for students who are just starting their journey.

At the same time, Women in STEM has gained new profiles highlighting women who shaped — and continue to shape — science, technology, engineering and mathematics across different countries and backgrounds.

What’s next?

Stories & Journeys will focus on personal paths in STEM — including my own projects, challenges, learning experiences, and stories from girls who started early and chose to build their future in science and engineering.

Resources will be a curated collection of books, films, documentaries, videos, competitions and learning materials related to STEM, aerospace and science culture.

These sections will grow gradually, alongside new profiles and articles, as Skyward She continues its mission to inspire curiosity, confidence and ambition.
